Spider-Man is a Marvel Comics superhero, the alter ego of Peter Parker, known for his spider-like abilities and iconic motto, "With great power comes great responsibility." Origin and Creation Spider-Man was created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko, first appearing in Amazing Fantasy #15 in 1962 during the Silver Age of Comic Books Wikipedia Wikipedia +1 . Peter Parker, a teenage orphan raised by Aunt May and Uncle Ben in Queens, New York, gains superhuman abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider Wikipedia Wikipedia +1 . Initially using his powers for personal gain, Peter learns the importance of responsibility after his Uncle Ben is killed by a burglar he could have stopped, inspiring his lifelong mission to fight crime
